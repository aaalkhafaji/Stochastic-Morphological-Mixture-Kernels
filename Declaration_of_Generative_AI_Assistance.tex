\section*{Declaration of generative AI assistance}
During the preparation of this manuscript, the authors used AI-assisted tools only for grammar correction, language readability, and formatting support. The AI tools were not used to generate scientific content, mathematical derivations, simulations, data, figures, results, analysis, interpretations, or conclusions. All content was reviewed, verified, and approved by the authors, who take full responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of the manuscript.
